Shorts Studio

A personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public. Updated November 6, 2026.

What it accesses

With your permission, Shorts Studio uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos you approve, set their thumbnails and publish times, and read view, like and comment counts for videos it uploaded. It also searches public YouTube videos to see what topics are popular.

Where your data goes

Nothing is sent anywhere except Google's YouTube API. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Shorts Studio folder on the computer removes everything it stored.
